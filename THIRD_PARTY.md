# Third-party assets

The game data (`engine/data`) contains assets made by others. They keep their own licences; the detailed notices
(in French) are in [`licences/`](licences).

| Assets | Authors / origin | Licence | Notice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Music: battle, boss, jingles, ambiences, race tracks, other worlds | OpenGameArt authors (HydroGene, Juhani Junkala, Emma_MA, cynicmusic, Kosmo The Cat, Kistol, JaggedStone, Paul Wortmann, MintoDog, section31, SRG774…) | CC0 1.0 | [`licences/musique.md`](licences/musique.md) |
| Music: story themes | generated with Google Gemini (Lyria) | AI-generated | [`licences/musique.md`](licences/musique.md) |
| Sound effects | Kenney, rubberduck and other OpenGameArt packs | CC0 1.0 | [`licences/sons.md`](licences/sons.md) |
| Sound effects | generated with Stable Audio Open 1.0 | Stability AI Community License. Powered by Stability AI | [`licences/sons.md`](licences/sons.md) |
| Interface fonts | Oxanium (Severin Meyer), Share Tech Mono (Carrois Apostrophe), from Google Fonts | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | [`licences/polices.md`](licences/polices.md) |
| Furniture 3D models | Kenney (Furniture Kit, Survival Kit) | CC0 1.0 | [`licences/modeles-3d.md`](licences/modeles-3d.md) |

Character sprites were generated with PixelLab; textures and illustrations with Stable Diffusion 1.5 community
checkpoints, run locally. The Poké Ball Plus input protocol follows
[pokeball-plus-4-windows](https://github.com/rna0/pokeball-plus-4-windows) (MIT); its command protocol is documented
in [pokeball-plus-re](https://github.com/Nerobeus/pokeball-plus-re).
